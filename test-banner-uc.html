Why does the banner look plain here

It does not. The Usercentrics banner renders inside a shadow root, and the page stylesheet is scoped to .page on top of that, so the banner shows exactly the styling configured in the admin interface.

Why are categories listed instead of fixed names

Usercentrics models consent per data processing service, and the category slugs come from the configuration. The monitor groups the services returned by UC_UI.getServicesBaseInfo by categorySlug, so it shows whatever this settings ID actually has.

Why are the embeds blocked before consent

The Smart Data Protector blocks known third party embeds by matching their source. It only works if the matching service, for example Google Maps or YouTube Video, is present in the configuration.

How do I bring the banner back

Use the first layer or second layer button in the click actions section, or the link in the footer.
