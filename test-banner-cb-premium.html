How do I prove the page is not styling the banner

Open the banner, then use Audit style isolation in the monitor. It walks every rule in this page stylesheet and counts how many of them match an element inside the banner or the declaration. The expected answer is zero. Read banner colours then prints the computed background and text colour of each button in the dialog, which is what you compare against the admin.

Why does this matter more than with other CMPs

Cookiebot renders the dialog in light DOM, as a child of body, so page selectors and inherited properties reach it. A CMP that renders inside a shadow root is protected by the browser. Here the protection has to come from how the stylesheet is written.

Why are the embeds not always blocked

This page deliberately does not set data-blockingmode, so the blocking mode is whatever the admin says. If you want automatic blocking regardless of the admin, add data-blockingmode="auto" to the loader tag.

How do I bring the banner back

Use the show banner again button in the click actions section, or the link in the footer. Both call Cookiebot.renew.
